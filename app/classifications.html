ⓘ How are industries and unclassified shipments calculated?

For Air/Ocean Export, the primary industry comes from the shipper. For Air/Ocean Import, it comes from the consignee. You can override the role using the Counterparty role filter. The commercial customer is a separate entity and is not used for primary industry attribution.

Unclassified counts shipment rows whose selected party has no matching saved industry. One company can contribute many unclassified shipments; it is not a count of unique companies. Click any industry, including Unclassified, to open its operational drilldown.

Percentages use all shipment rows in the selected company, department and date range. The latest monthly change may compare incomplete months.

Industry attributed by shipment direction: exports use shipper; imports use consignee. Customer is a separate commercial entity.
