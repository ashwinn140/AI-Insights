From shipment data to business decisions

A practical guide to interpreting the platform, understanding what each visual tells you, and turning operational evidence into better commercial and management decisions.

Important boundary: AI Insights is an operational intelligence and industry-research tool. Shipment activity is not revenue, gross profit, margin, customer profitability or confirmed sales pipeline. Validate important conclusions against source records and your finance/CRM systems.

1. What business outcomes does AI Insights support?

Operational visibility

Understand shipment workload, monthly movement, business-unit mix, branches and trade lanes. Helps leaders spot concentration, workload shifts and areas requiring operational review.

Customer and network intelligence

Identify shipper and consignee organisations that appear in the operational dataset, understand their observed participation and map geographic/lane relationships.

Industry segmentation

Organise organisations into a consistent 15-vertical taxonomy. This supports sector-level customer analysis, market coverage assessment and focused commercial research.

Commercial research prioritisation

Use repeated shipment appearances and lane breadth to identify organisations worth researching. These are signals for investigation—not evidence of buying intent or a sales opportunity.

Sales coverage insight

Review shipment activity by the ERP Sales Person field and industry. Helps understand observed sector coverage and identify gaps for management discussion.

Data quality and governance

Use pending classification, evidence URLs, confidence and research verification statuses to focus data-quality work and make classification decisions traceable.

2. Operations Overview

KPI tiles

  • Shipment records: count of filtered rows in the approved operational source; use as workload/volume context.
  • Shipper appearances: non-empty shipper records, not unique customers.
  • Consignee appearances: non-empty consignee records, not unique customers.
  • Total TEUs: sum of ERP-reported TEUs; interpret as ocean container-equivalent volume, not shipment count.

Monthly shipment trend

Shows shipment rows grouped by shipment month. Use to identify seasonality, growth/decline and unusual movement. Compare equivalent date ranges before drawing conclusions.

Activity by department

Compares shipment workload by business unit and department (Air Export, Air Import, Ocean Export, Ocean Import). Supports resource and operating-mix reviews.

Top trade lanes

Ranks origin/destination pairs by shipment rows. Use to identify important corridors, network concentration and possible route-development priorities.

Company participation

Lists shipper and consignee name appearances. ERP spellings may differ; appearances should not be treated as deduplicated legal entities.

Branch distribution

Compares recorded shipment rows by branch. Supports branch workload and capacity discussions.

Data coverage

Earliest/latest shipment dates and gross weight provide coverage context. Gross weight is shown in source ERP units; confirm unit conventions before comparing.

Cross-filters are designed to apply consistently across the KPI tiles, charts, tables and Excel export. If a visual appears inconsistent, reset filters and reproduce the issue before relying on the result.

3. Industry Classification & Intelligence

Classified organisations

Organisations with a saved nonblank industry. Used to measure segmentation coverage.

Pending classification

Organisations without an active industry assignment. Pending means no industry is assigned; there is no approval queue.

Industry contribution and trend

Shipment rows attributed to saved industry classifications over time. Use to compare observed sector activity, not sector revenue or profitability.

Salesperson × industry

Uses the ERP Sales Person field for commercial attribution. Job Owner is a separate operational field and must not be substituted.

Evidence and confidence

Use credible company-specific evidence, rationale and confidence. If evidence is insufficient or ambiguous, leave the industry pending rather than guessing.

Workbook workflow

Sheet 1 is the import-ready data sheet; Sheet 2 contains the AI prompt and canonical taxonomy. Only valid canonical labels should be imported; unresolved rows remain pending.

Export shipment industry attribution uses shipper for export and consignee for import. A company name appearing in ERP is not, by itself, proof of its primary industry.

4. Company Explorer

Searches shipper and consignee names in the authorised shipment source. Role, appearances and distinct lane breadth help explain how an organisation participates in the observed network. Use it to validate account presence and understand route exposure. Names are ERP-recorded strings and may contain spelling variants; do not assume one row equals one legal entity.

5. Activity-based Discovery

Ranks organisations using observed shipment appearances and distinct trade-lane breadth. It is a research shortlist—not a verified prospect list, sales pipeline, buying-intent score, or forecast. Before outreach, verify legal identity, current operations, fit, contacts and commercial relevance independently.

6. Research Directory

Stores research entities, canonical/parent relationships, country, business activity, industry, sub-industry, rationale, evidence URL, confidence and verification status. Treat provisional, probable and unresolved records according to their stated status. The research master is a separate evidence dataset and does not silently overwrite manually saved classifications.

7. Administration

Administrators manage users and authorised company/department scopes, inspect audit events, review source reconciliation and manage controlled research imports. Shipment analytics remain read-only. The reconciliation view checks aggregate source counts and selected missing fields; it is not an independent reconciliation against a separate shipment register.

8. How to interpret the numbers responsibly

  • Rows vs entities: shipment rows and appearances are activity counts, not unique legal entities or customers.
  • Filters matter: compare like-for-like company, department, date and lane scopes.
  • Attribution: export activity is associated with shipper; import activity with consignee. Salesperson comes from the ERP Sales Person field.
  • Classification coverage: sector totals depend on saved classifications; pending organisations are not included as a confirmed industry.
  • Data limitations: spelling variants, missing fields, source latency and incomplete date coverage can affect interpretation.
  • Financial boundary: this application does not access profit lines or calculate revenue, margin, cost, GST or customer profitability.
  • Action: use the signal to form a question, drill into the underlying records, validate it, then make the management or commercial decision.
